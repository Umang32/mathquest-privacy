MathQuest Privacy Policy

RAM Technology
Updated 22 November 2026
Contact: dev@ksumiyo.com

Who we are

MathQuest is an offline maths adventure published by RAM Technology. This policy describes this version of MathQuest for children and families. A parent or guardian can contact us at dev@ksumiyo.com about privacy.

Information used on your device

MathQuest stores game progress, chapter checkpoints, puzzle responses, scores, earned coins, collectibles and sound, motion and other preferences locally on your device. This information is used to run the game and resume your adventure. The app does not request a name, email address, age, account, location, contacts, camera or microphone.

Collection and sharing

The game does not send your gameplay or preferences to RAM Technology or third parties. It contains no advertising, analytics, social features, chat, user-generated content or tracking SDKs. Fonts, illustrations and audio are bundled with the app. Gameplay does not require an internet connection.

Children and families

Children can use MathQuest without registering or submitting personal information. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through the game. If you believe a child has shared personal information with us through support, please contact dev@ksumiyo.com so we can address it.

Google Play and payments

Google Play handles downloading the app, any price charged for the download and related payment details under Google's own policies. RAM Technology does not receive your card details through MathQuest. This version has no in-app purchases or subscriptions. The coins and collectibles in the game are earned by playing and have no cash value. Google Play may separately process store, device, purchase or diagnostic information under your Google settings and its own policies.

Support messages

If you choose to email RAM Technology, we receive the email address and information you include. We use it to answer your request and keep it only as long as needed for support or applicable obligations. Please do not send children's names, sensitive information or payment details. Support email is optional and is not sent automatically by the app.

Storage, deletion and device changes

Game data remains on your device until you clear MathQuest's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. This release disables Android app backup and excludes its local files from Android backup and device-transfer rules. It has no developer-operated cloud save or account recovery. Clearing app data or uninstalling deletes local progress. Store purchase records are managed separately by Google Play.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy when the app's data practices change. The date above identifies this version. For questions or requests about information you have sent to support, contact RAM Technology at dev@ksumiyo.com.
